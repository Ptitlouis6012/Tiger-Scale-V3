# v3.9.0

Your TigerScale now speaks Russian and Dutch, on the screen, in its web page and
in the installer, and every language now writes its time units in its own words.

## Added

- **Russian and Dutch.** Both appear at the end of Settings > Language, so a scale
  that already has a language keeps it. The scale's web page and the web
  installer offer them too: eleven languages everywhere, the same list as Tiger
  Studio.
- **See and drive the screen from a browser.** Open `http://<your scale>/screen`
  on the same network and you get the picture on the panel; clicking presses it,
  dragging swipes. The same thing is available as `/screen.bmp` and
  `/api/tap?x=&y=` for scripts. It needs no setup and no access code, which also
  means anyone on your local network can use it; an injected press never acts on
  a sleeping screen, it only wakes it, exactly as a finger would.

## Changed

- **"Account", not "Firebase".** The web page's sign-in messages now say
  "Account credentials saved" and "Account error"; Firebase is how the account
  works, not something you should have to know about.
- **Spanish questions and exclamations open with ¿ and ¡**, as Spanish is
  written.
- **Minutes and hours are translated.** The screen-sleep delay and the account
  page's "last update" age used to read "min" and "h" in every language; they now
  read "мин", "分钟", "Min." and so on.

## Fixed

- **The web page shows the same weight as the screen when its live connection
  drops.** It falls back to polling the scale, and that path used to round
  anything under 5 g down to 0 g, while the screen showed the real, sometimes
  negative, figure.

## Known issues

- A tag programmed with the TigerTag Android app can be stored without the
  leading `0` of its ID, so the scale does not recognise the spool and creates a
  second one with no brand or material (#36). The fix belongs in the app.
- Scrolling the Settings list with a finger feels sluggish (#38). Measured and
  understood: every step of a scroll repaints the whole screen. Not fixed yet.

## Updating

Over the air from **Settings > Update**, or over USB with
`bash scripts/flash.sh --fs`. Both keep your WiFi credentials, TigerTag session
and calibration.
